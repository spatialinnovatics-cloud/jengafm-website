About JengaFM

A single point of contact for every aspect of your building

JengaFM was built around one idea: facilities management shouldn't mean juggling a dozen contractors. We bring Hard FM, Soft FM and every specialist trade under one nationwide contract — one helpdesk, one set of SLAs, one accountable partner.

Hard & Soft FM, unified

From M&E and fabric maintenance to cleaning and grounds care, we deliver Hard FM and Soft FM as one integrated service — or as a fully managed Total Facilities Management (TFM) contract.

Compliance-first

Statutory compliance, certification, and risk management are built into every contract, so your buildings stay safe, legal, and audit-ready.

Nationwide, 24/7

A national engineer network and 24/7 helpdesk mean consistent response times whether you operate one site or a hundred.

Who We Support

Every sector, every building type

We support offices, retail, industrial sites, healthcare, education, hospitality, residential blocks, warehouses, and public-sector buildings across the country.

Our approach

We start by understanding your estate — its assets, its compliance obligations, and its operational rhythm. From there we design a maintenance and service plan that consolidates as many trades as possible under one contract, reducing the number of suppliers you manage and the risk of gaps between them.
